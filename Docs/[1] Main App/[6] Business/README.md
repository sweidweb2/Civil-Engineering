## Marketplace correction — LOCKED

Applies to every batch:

- Provider side: publish/display goods, materials, equipment, services, prices, and relevant information only.
- Client/guest: browse/view and contact the provider directly or visit physically.
- PM: browse for planning/reference; assign/reference resources **inside the project** only.
- No orders, rental transactions, checkout/payment, accept/reject, or transaction pipeline on the platform.
- Browsing creates no technical or commercial relationship between roles.
- Marketplace items stay directory records owned by the provider.
- Provider quantity/availability is informational only.
- Real-world purchasing, renting, hiring, negotiation, payment, delivery, and communication happen outside the platform.

**Marketplace:** Discover → View information → Contact externally.

**PM project:** Plan → Assign/reference → Calculate → Record actual expenses.
